How fishing works

    

      Craft a coarse net, then speak to the Wise Old Man in the derelict building in Bramblemead Valley to start
      Shrimp Catcher — that unlocks the skill. Nets and rods are not interchangeable: nets only work on net spots
      (shoals of small fish), rods only on rod spots (the bigger ripples).
    

    

      Hold the cast button to charge. Too weak or too strong and the cast misses, but it costs you no stamina and
      no bait. Once a fish bites, strafe against it — hold A when it pulls right, D when it pulls left. The fish bar
      turns red when it changes direction. Reel only when it tires, or you burn stamina for nothing.
    

    

      The advice that saves the most frustration: net fish first, to around level 20. Rod fishing is stamina-hungry
      and the early attempts mostly fail. You need the shrimp for bait anyway.
    

  


  

    
Bait

    

      Bait is optional and you can cast without it, but junk — stones, weeds — becomes far more likely.
      Shrimp bait is the easy starting option, and shrimp are among the simplest net catches, so the loop feeds
      itself. Feathered lures are the ones named for salmon. Beyond those two the per-fish bait table isn't
      documented anywhere I could verify, so it's genuinely a case of experimenting per region.
    

    

      The general rule from the wiki is to match the region: carry that region's rod and its matching bait.
